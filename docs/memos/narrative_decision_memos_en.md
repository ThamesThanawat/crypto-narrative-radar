## MEMO 1: Exchange Tokens Narrative

**Snapshot date:** 2026-05-20
**Flag:** Skew Review
**Reason:** Mean-median gap of 3.0pp meets the ≥3.0pp review threshold.

### Observations

- Exchange Tokens ranks #1 in Narrative Momentum Score at 76.9 and
  is the only narrative with a positive mean 7D return at +0.4%.
- Median 7D return is −2.6%, diverging from the mean. The basket is
  not as broadly strong as the headline score suggests.
- Primary driver: HYPE at +31.5%, representing 54.9% of total basket
  volume.
- The remaining 9 tokens all posted negative 7D returns, ranging from
  −2.1% to −9.5%.

### Interpretation

Exchange Tokens ranks #1 because HYPE outperformed, not because
demand was distributed across the basket.

The score carries high basket-representativeness risk. Using this
ranking as evidence that the Exchange Tokens sector has a broad
tailwind would be a misread — the majority of basket tokens do not
confirm the same signal.

In a listing review context, a project claiming Exchange Tokens
narrative fit should not receive credit from the group score directly,
unless its own liquidity profile, volume quality, and tokenomics
support that claim independently.

### Decision Implication

Discount the Exchange Tokens #1 ranking as a sector signal for this
snapshot.

Projects claiming exchange token narrative tailwind must be evaluated
on their own liquidity profile, volume quality, tokenomics, and
demand drivers — not by reference to the basket average.

### View Changes When

At least 6 of 10 basket tokens post positive 7D returns without HYPE
as the sole driver.

On-chain confirmation that HYPE's volume reflects organic activity —
not wash trading or flow concentrated in a small number of wallets —
would directly strengthen or weaken the HYPE-specific thesis.

## MEMO 2: RWA Narrative

**Snapshot date:** 2026-05-20
**Flag:** Dispersion Review
**Reason:** 7D return IQR of 12.1pp is approximately 3× the snapshot
median IQR, exceeding the ≥2× review threshold.

### Observations

- RWA ranks #4 in Narrative Momentum Score at 68.8 and has the
  broadest participation breadth in this snapshot at 40.0%.
- Mean 7D return is −4.9%. Mean-median gap is 1.9pp — within normal
  range. This is not a skew problem.
- The issue is internal dispersion. POLYX is the top 7D token at
  +5.4%; GFI is the bottom at −21.6%.
- The spread between top and bottom basket tokens is 26.9pp,
  indicating that RWA groups projects with materially different
  market behaviors under a single label.

### Interpretation

The RWA label is too broad to serve as a reliable decision signal in
this snapshot.

POLYX and GFI are not responding to the same market signal. POLYX
is closer to tokenized securities infrastructure — compliance layers,
institutional onboarding, and assets requiring regulatory alignment.
GFI is closer to real-world credit and lending protocols, with higher
exposure to credit risk, liquidity risk, and risk-off conditions.

As a result, the 40.0% breadth figure carries limited weight. Broad
participation does not imply coherent sector movement when basket
components are driven by different underlying demand.

In a listing review context, projects applying under RWA must be
matched to the correct sub-category before evaluation. The aggregate
basket score is not a sufficient benchmark.

### Decision Implication

Do not use the RWA Narrative Momentum Score as a single valuation
reference in listing review.

Projects claiming RWA narrative fit must be evaluated against their
specific sub-category peers — tokenized securities, real-world credit,
or institutional DeFi infrastructure — not against the full RWA
basket.

### View Changes When

IQR compresses below 2× the snapshot median IQR, and returns begin
moving coherently across multiple sub-categories.
